# Leave-by and trips (v1.5.10/build47)

## Product authority

The owner's "Calendar runs the house" story (frame 1 of both stories, Leave-by and Travel day) asked for the Mac side on 2026-11-21. Glimpse only reads the calendar; house actions stay on the phone (Hearth).

## Change

**Menu bar:**
- A new opt-in setting, "Show leave-by time" (off by default), extends the next-meeting segment.
- When today's next meeting has a physical place, the item reads "Dentist · leave by 8:40". Leave-by is the start minus a buffer of 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes (default 20, the same as Hearth).
- It turns amber 5 minutes before leave-by, the same amber as the existing threshold. An amber leave-by wins over a meeting in progress, as the imminent countdown already does.
- Once leave-by passes, it falls back to "Dentist in 12m". Events without a place keep the old behaviour.
- URLs and call placeholders ("Zoom", "Microsoft Teams Meeting", "Google Meet" and similar) don't count as places. There are no maps, no traffic lookups and no network calls.
- Still one composited image through `MenuBarItemRenderer`, with no status-button subview.

**Agenda:**
- A located event that hasn't started shows an amber "Leave by 8:40" chip while the setting is on. Its subtitle reads "9:00 AM · 412 Oak St".
- A located event covering 2 or more calendar days shows a neutral "Away 5 days" chip and the subtitle "5 days · South Lake Tahoe" whenever its day is selected. This doesn't depend on the setting.
- The chips are part of the row Button, so Tab order, hover and Return/Space don't change. The row's accessibility label adds the place and the chip ("…, 412 Oak St, Leave by 8:40 AM"), and the chip has its own label.

**Settings (Menu bar):**
- The Meetings row is one line: a "Show leave-by time" checkbox with a trailing buffer menu ("20 min"), disabled while the checkbox is off.
- "Next meeting" moves into the "Menu bar shows" choices (same accessibility label and help), on the Year line, so leave-by adds no row.
- The Hearth footnote was dropped. The Settings height stays at 630pt: the document is 588.5pt locally in the tallest update states (585pt before leave-by). A first version with a separate row and footnote measured 627pt locally and 653pt on the hosted runners, where the clip is 622pt (known issue 5).

**Not done:** the trip tint on the month grid. The agenda fetches events only for the selected day, so a tint would need a new month-range EventKit query and per-cell state in the grid. That isn't cheap, so it was skipped.

## Verification

- **Code:** an independent review against A1–A8 found no CRITICAL or HIGH issues. One MEDIUM was fixed: before the first load there was no load time, and a leave-by chip could still show. Now no leave-by chip shows until the first load, while trips still do. One staleness was accepted: the agenda chips reflect the last load, and reopening the panel refreshes them.
- **QA:**
  - GlimpseCore: 303 tests in 27 suites, including the new `LeaveByTests` and `LeaveByFeatureTests` (TestStore, fixed dates).
  - App: 424 tests in 53 suites, with the opt-in keyboard and agenda-refresh suites skipped locally.
  - Release tooling: 239 tests.
  - The keyboard and agenda manifests are unchanged, because no methods were added to them.
- **Performance:** no new queries. The leave-by moments are computed from the cached events on the existing minute tick, and the agenda chip is computed once per load.
- **UX:** renders in light and dark (upcoming, amber, after leave-by) of the menu bar and of the agenda chips (Leave by and Away 5 days) were inspected against the mock.
